CLOUD AND JENKINS - STUDENT MATERIALS

Before class:
  Read 01_Before_Class\Student_Prerequisites_Standalone.pdf.
  Install/check the tools and complete the initial Jenkins setup.
  You do not need the project to follow that handout.

When the instructor introduces the assignment:
  Read 02_Assignment\Student_Assignment.pdf first.
  Use Student_Lab_Guide_Graded.pdf for setup/troubleshooting.
  Extract FestPass_Java_Project.zip and Jenkins_Laptop_Setup.zip into
  the SAME parent directory so festpass-java and jenkins-lab are siblings.
  Reuse your prepared CloudJenkinsLab folder, preserving downloads/runtime.

The application intentionally fails tests. Your job is to configure the
trigger, diagnose the failure, fix the app, and demonstrate automatic green.
The source, tests, and existing pipeline stages are provided.
No new Java feature or tests are required.

Use your project Git author name for your alias. The instructor supplies
the class and challenge codes. Read the brief's build evidence notice.

The assessed work takes about one hour AFTER environment preparation.
